The AI Bidding Engine

The core logic heavily utilizes behavior-weighting. Each of the 10 franchises operates using a dedicated `personality profile` (e.g., RCB is coded to prioritize star batters, SRH targets all-rounders). The engine scales `max_bid` tolerances dynamically based on real-time **Auction Pressure** (how many teams are fighting) and **Role Scarcity** (how badly a team needs a specific role).
